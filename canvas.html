Credits and download

Keep your four credits for the class wall

For each picture, paste its exact credit line from the Class Image Bank. Match the credit slot to the frame where you used that picture. These stay on this device while you work.

Open the class wall →

When all four slots are filled, copy them, then paste them underneath your comic on the class wall.

Describe your comic for the class wall

Write one plain sentence saying what happens across the four frames. When you post, paste it into the Padlet image description so a reader who cannot see the picture can follow the joke too.

Quick name check: are all names made-up characters — not students, teachers, friends or anyone you know?

Clear every frame, its credits and its comic description? You can undo once, until you refresh.
