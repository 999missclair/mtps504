Class image bank

Class Image Bank

Pictures that are already safe to use. The credit line is written for you — copy it for your class-wall post.

Twelve pictures — one for each character you can roll. Find your character below, copy the credit line under the picture, and keep it for your class-wall post. Every picture here is free to use, so nobody has to read a licence box today.

Want to search the open web instead? That’s the extra option on 4 Build →.

Use a bank picture in three steps

  1. Press Download picture on the one you want. It goes to Downloads.
  2. Press Copy beside its exact credit line.
  3. Open the Comic Builder, choose the frame, press Add a picture, then paste the credit into that frame’s credit slot.

Do one frame at a time. The picture and its credit stay together.
