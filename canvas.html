Why the GIFs stay in class

GIFs are mostly clips from TV and film — somebody else’s work. In class, for planning, you can use them. They don’t go on the public gallery — your finished comic there uses open-licence pictures, the ones you can name. This is the same rule you sorted on 4 Build.
