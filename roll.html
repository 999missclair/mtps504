4 · Put it on the board

Everyone’s roll goes up. You’ll see everyone else’s, and at least one of them will make you think of something for yours.

  1. Paste your brief.
  2. Put it in the column you think is carrying the joke — the who, the where, or the problem.
  3. Say which card you picked and why, in one line.
  4. Read someone else’s and reply with one “what if…”.

Post as your first name and your table number. No surnames. Sam T4 is perfect.

! Reply to one other roll with a “what if…”

Not “that’s good”. Not “lol”. One idea they could use: what if the lights came back on too early? What if the tour group was in every frame?

Stuck? Try this

Three that already work:

  • A rabbit with a sword · an empty plinth appears next door · the description card is a lie
  • A suit of armour · being packed into a crate · someone has touched them
  • A monkey dressed as a painter · the lights go out · their colours are fading

Going further

Add one of your own: pick an object that has to appear in all four frames. Now the repeat is built in and frame 4 has something to break.

What good looks like

Read your four lines back. Tick everything that’s true.

  • My four frames tell one story, in order.
  • Frame 4 is the funny one — the surprise lands last.
  • I’ve used at least two different shot types.
  • Something repeats across my frames, and one thing changes at the end.
  • Each frame has one line I could put under the picture.
  • I can name the licence of every image I’m planning to use.

Anything you didn’t tick is your next job. Nobody ticks all six first go.
